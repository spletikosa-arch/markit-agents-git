# Campaign Brief: Planerio Q1 Product Launch

## Objective

Drive **1,000 trial signups in 30 days** from distributed team managers at tech companies (50-500 employees). Secondary objective: establish "smart productivity coordination for distributed teams" as Planerio's category position ahead of Clockwise, RescueTime, and Reclaim.ai.

## Target Audience

Primary: **Manager Maria** — distributed engineering/team managers, 35-45, coordinating 10-15 people across time zones. Secondary audiences: Founder Fiona (early-stage founders solving this problem without an ops function) and Director Dana (marketing directors who need team-level, not individual-level, focus-time tools).

See [personas](../../brand/personas/) for full detail.

### A Day in the Life of Manager Maria

Maria's day starts before the workday officially does — checking Slack for anything that came in overnight from the team members in Europe, three hours ahead. By 10am she's in back-to-back 1:1s and sprint syncs, most of them existing not because the work demands them, but because a live conversation is the only reliable way to coordinate across a team split over three time zones. By early afternoon, when her US-based developers finally overlap with her European team's tail end, that narrow window fills instantly with meetings — squeezing out the only stretch of the day when the whole team could theoretically do focused work together. She ends most days having spent more of it in calendar Tetris than in anything that moved the roadmap forward.

### Specific Pain Points We're Addressing

- **The overlap window gets consumed by meetings, not protected for deep work.** The one time slot her whole distributed team shares becomes the default dumping ground for every sync, leaving no shared quiet time.
- **Manual coordination doesn't scale.** Finding a focus block that works across time zones means she's personally checking multiple calendars every week — a job Planerio can do automatically.
- **She wants visibility, not surveillance.** Existing tools she's tried (or considered) skew toward individual tracking, which reads as monitoring rather than support — a fast way to lose team trust.

### Why Now

Distributed and hybrid teams aren't a temporary phase — they're the default at the 50-500 employee tech companies Planerio targets, and the coordination tax only grows as teams add more time zones. Maria is actively feeling this pain today, not hypothetically: every sprint, she loses real focus time to scheduling friction that a tool built specifically for distributed coordination — not adapted from individual time-tracking or calendar optimization — can solve immediately. The 14-day trial and Slack/Zoom/Google Workspace integrations mean she can test this within her existing stack, this week, without a migration project competing for her already-scarce time.

## Key Messages

Drawn from the [brand voice guide](../../brand/voice-guide.md):

1. **Coordination, not control.** Planerio aligns teams on focus time without policing individual calendars.
2. **Built for distributed teams, not adapted for them.** Time zones and async handoffs are the starting assumption.
3. **AI that removes work, not adds a dashboard.** Fewer meetings, less manual scheduling — not another tool to check.
4. **Fast to value.** Live in minutes via Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace. No migration project.

All copy follows brand voice: benefits before features, "we/you" perspective, no hyperbole or jargon, active voice.
