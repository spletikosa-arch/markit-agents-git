# Q2 Growth Campaign — Timeline

> Draft timeline. Finalize start date once Q1 wrap-up report is complete — see exercises/markit/campaigns/q1-launch/.

## Phase 1: Q1 Wrap & Setup (Week 1-2)
- Compile Q1 performance report (see exercises/markit/analytics/)
- Identify Q1 trial cohort for retention/expansion outreach
- Recruit 2-3 Q1 customers for case study interviews

## Phase 2: Content & Proof Points (Week 3-5)
- Publish 2 case studies
- Launch retention email sequence to non-converted Q1 trials
- Begin organic LinkedIn push featuring customer outcomes

## Phase 3: Expansion Push (Week 6-9)
- Team-expansion email nudges to paid accounts
- Host first community webinar
- Mid-campaign performance review, reallocate budget to best-performing channel

## Phase 4: Close & Report (Week 10-12)
- Final push on trial signups
- Compile Q2 performance report
- Feed learnings into Q3 planning

## Dependencies
- Q1 actuals (spend, CAC, conversion rate) required before Phase 1 can be finalized with real numbers
- Case study customers must be identified and confirmed before Phase 2 content production starts
