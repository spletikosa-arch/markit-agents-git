# Q2 Growth Campaign — KPIs & Tracking

## Primary KPI
- **2,500 trial signups** across the quarter

## Secondary KPIs
- Trial-to-paid conversion: **≥20%** (up from Q1 target of ≥15%)
- Cost per trial signup: at or below Q1 actual (pending Q1 data)
- 2 published case studies live
- Net-new expansion revenue from Q1 paid accounts (target TBD)
- Webinar: registrant count and attendee-to-trial conversion rate (new metric for Q2, no baseline yet)

## Tracking Plan
- Signups and conversion: same attribution setup as Q1 — see exercises/markit/analytics/ once Q1 report exists
- Case study performance: page views + assisted conversions (multi-touch, not last-click only — case studies typically influence rather than directly convert)
- Email: open rate, CTR, and conversion-to-trial-reactivation rate for the retention sequence specifically

## Review Cadence
- Weekly channel-level check-in
- Mid-campaign full review at Week 6 (see timeline.md)
- Final report at campaign close, feeding into Q3 planning
