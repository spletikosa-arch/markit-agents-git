# Campaign Brief: Planerio Q2 Growth Campaign

## Objective

Convert Q1 launch momentum into sustainable growth: **2,500 trial signups** and improve trial-to-paid conversion from the Q1 baseline (target ≥15%) to **20%** over the quarter, by focusing on retention-driving content and expansion within existing accounts' teams.

## Target Audience

Same core personas as Q1 ([Manager Maria](../../brand/personas/manager-maria.md), [Founder Fiona](../../brand/personas/founder-fiona.md), [Director Dana](../../brand/personas/director-dana.md)), with a shift in emphasis toward Director Dana — Q1 data (once available) should confirm whether marketing-led teams convert at a higher rate and justify the increased focus.

## Key Messages

Same four pillars from the [brand voice guide](../../brand/voice-guide.md), with Q2 adding a proof-point layer: real usage data and early customer outcomes from Q1 trial cohorts, since "coordination not control" is easier to prove with results than to assert from a launch campaign.

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Content (SEO) | Sustained organic demand | Case studies from Q1 trial customers, comparison pages matured with real usage data |
| Email | Retention + expansion | Lifecycle emails targeting Q1 trial users who didn't convert; team-expansion nudges for paid accounts |
| LinkedIn | Continued awareness | Shift budget toward organic + customer story amplification, less pure paid reach |
| Webinar/Community | New for Q2 | Live session for distributed team managers — positions Markit/Planerio as a resource, not just a vendor |
