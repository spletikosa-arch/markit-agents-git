# Persona: Founder Fiona

## Demographics & Role
- **Age:** 28-35
- **Title:** Co-founder / CEO
- **Company:** Early-to-growth-stage startup, roughly 10-50 employees, often remote-first
- **Team:** Small, distributed, everyone wearing multiple hats

## Pain Points & Frustrations
- Her calendar is fragmented across fundraising, hiring, customer calls, and product decisions — there's no clean line between "her time" and "the company's time."
- The team is remote across a handful of time zones, and there's no formal ops function to manage coordination — it falls to her by default.
- She's acutely aware that every hour lost to scheduling friction is an hour not spent on the handful of things that actually move the company forward.
- Tool fatigue is real: she's cautious about adding another subscription unless it clearly pays for itself fast.

## Goals & Motivations
- Protect focus time for herself and the team without hiring an ops person just to manage calendars.
- Move fast — she needs tools that work in minutes, not tools that need a rollout plan.
- Build habits now (as a 10-person team) that won't need to be re-architected at 50 or 100 people.
- Keep the team aligned and unblocked despite being distributed and thinly staffed.

## Current Tools
- Google Workspace, Slack, Zoom — the default early-startup stack
- Notion for documentation, Linear for product tracking
- No dedicated scheduling or focus-time tool yet — she's been solving this with manual Slack messages ("let's block Thursday afternoons")

## How Planerio Helps Her
- Sets up in minutes through tools she already uses — no onboarding project, no new habits to teach a small team.
- Automates what she's currently doing manually (asking the team to self-organize focus blocks), freeing her from being the de facto ops person.
- Scales with the company — the coordination problem gets harder as she hires, and Planerio is built for distributed teams from the start, not retrofitted.
- $12/user/month with a 14-day trial is a low-risk way to test it before committing spend she has to justify to a co-founder or investor.

## Information Sources She Trusts
- Twitter/X and LinkedIn posts from other founders she follows
- Y Combinator / startup community forums and Slack groups
- Product Hunt launches
- Direct recommendations from her founder peer group — she moves fast on trusted word-of-mouth over formal sales process
