# Planerio Brand Voice Guide

> Source of truth for how Planerio sounds across every channel. When in doubt, check this file before writing.

## Brand Personality

Planerio talks like a smart colleague who respects your time — not a productivity guru trying to sell you a system. We're the calm, competent teammate who's already thought through the coordination problem so your team doesn't have to.

**Professional but approachable.** We know our stuff, but we're not stiff about it. Think "senior engineer explaining a fix," not "consultant pitching a framework."

## Tone of Voice

- **Knowledgeable without being condescending.** Explain the "why," don't lecture. Assume the reader is smart and busy — respect both.
- **Direct.** Say the thing. Don't wind up to it.
- **Warm, not cute.** Approachable doesn't mean jokey. Save personality for a well-placed line, not every sentence.
- **Confident, not hypey.** We back claims with specifics, not superlatives.

## Perspective

Write in first/second person: **"we" and "you."** Planerio is a partner in the room, not a vendor issuing statements.

- ✅ "We noticed your team's focus time keeps getting fragmented. Here's how you fix that."
- ❌ "Planerio's proprietary algorithm optimizes organizational focus time allocation."

## Messaging Pillars

1. **Coordination, not control.** Planerio helps teams align on focus time — it doesn't police anyone's calendar. Managers get visibility; individuals keep autonomy.
2. **Built for distributed teams, not adapted for them.** Time zones, async handoffs, and scattered calendars are the starting assumption, not an edge case.
3. **AI that removes work, not adds a dashboard.** The value is fewer meetings and less manual scheduling — not another tool to check.
4. **Fast to value.** Setup takes minutes via Slack, Zoom, and Google Workspace. No migration project required.

## Focus: Benefits Before Features

Lead with what changes for the reader. Follow with the mechanism, briefly.

- ✅ "Your team gets four uninterrupted hours a day, automatically blocked around the meetings that actually need to happen."
- ❌ "Planerio uses AI-driven calendar analysis to generate optimized focus blocks."

## Words to Use

we / you / your team / focus time / coordinate / distributed / automatically / together / clarity / breathing room

## Words to Avoid

- **Hyperbole:** "revolutionary," "game-changing," "10x," "unlock your potential"
- **Jargon:** "synergy," "leverage" (as a verb), "streamline," "seamless," "best-in-class," "robust," "cutting-edge"
- **Passive voice:** "Focus time can be coordinated by teams" → "Teams coordinate focus time"
- **Guilt/hustle framing:** don't shame people for being "unproductive." Planerio is about protecting time, not extracting more of it.

## Writing Style Rules

- Short sentences for key points. Longer ones only when an idea genuinely needs the room.
- One idea per sentence. Cut the second clause if it's just restating the first.
- Numbers and specifics beat adjectives ("14-day trial," not "generous trial").
- Contractions are fine and preferred in most content — they read as human, not corporate.
- Avoid rhetorical questions as a crutch; use them only when they earn their place.

## Example: Good vs. Bad Copy

**Bad:**
> Planerio leverages cutting-edge AI to seamlessly streamline your organization's productivity ecosystem, empowering distributed teams to unlock unprecedented levels of synergy and focus.

**Good:**
> Your team is spread across four time zones and three calendars nobody fully controls. Planerio finds the focus time that's already there — and blocks it automatically, so meetings stop eating the hours your team actually needs to do the work.

**Bad (passive, hedgy):**
> It is recommended that teams consider adopting a more structured approach to time management in order to potentially improve outcomes.

**Good (direct, benefit-first):**
> Block four hours of real focus time a day. No extra meetings to schedule it — Planerio does that part.
