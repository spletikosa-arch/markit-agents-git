# Brand Folder — Contents Summary

## voice-guide.md
Planerio's brand voice reference: personality (professional but approachable), tone rules, "we/you" perspective, four messaging pillars (coordination not control, built for distributed teams, AI that removes work, fast to value), words to use/avoid, writing style rules, and good-vs-bad copy examples. Check this before writing anything customer-facing.

## personas/manager-maria.md
Primary target persona. Distributed engineering manager, 35-45, managing 10-15 developers across time zones. Core pain: manual cross-timezone scheduling and wariness of surveillance-feeling tools. Trusts peer recommendations and case studies with real metrics.

## personas/founder-fiona.md
Secondary persona. Startup founder, 28-35, solving team coordination without a dedicated ops function. Core motivator: speed of setup and low-risk trial before committing spend. Trusts founder peer networks and Product Hunt.

## personas/director-dana.md
Secondary persona. Marketing director, 40-50, at a mid-size company managing a distributed marketing team. Core motivator: needs to prove focus-time impact on campaign output to leadership with real metrics, not vibes. Trusts marketing ops communities and analyst reports.
